Nextora AI Credit Suite for NBFCs

Plug-and-play predictive AI models engineered to plug directly into your Loan Origination System (LOS) & Loan Management System (LMS) to cut NPAs and accelerate approvals.

MODEL 01

NexScore™ AI Underwriting Engine

Alternative-data credit scoring model combining bureau tradelines, GST/bank statement cash-flow velocity, and behavioral signals to score thin-file & MSME borrowers accurately.

  • Real-time API decisioning (< 400ms)
  • Explainable AI reason codes for credit officers
  • +24% approval lift on thin-file NTC applicants
MODEL 02

NexGuard™ Fraud & EWS Detector

Pre-disbursement anomaly detection and post-disbursement Early Warning System (EWS) that flags synthetic identities, tampered bank PDFs, and circular transactions.

  • Forensic bank statement & KYC tampering checks
  • Device & velocity ring-fencing alerts
  • Reduces first-payment default (FPD) by up to 38%
MODEL 03

NexCollect™ Propensity-to-Pay AI

Predictive delinquency segmentation model that forecasts DPD bucket roll-forwards and recommends optimal outreach channels, timing, and restructuring offers.

  • 30/60/90 DPD roll-rate probability scoring
  • Smart collection agent prioritization
  • Cuts collection ops cost by 28%
